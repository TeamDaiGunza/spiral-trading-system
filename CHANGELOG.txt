--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 24, 2026 (Afternoon) — Accuracy Degradation Fix
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ROOT CAUSE
  The bot was getting worse over long sessions because suppression logic was
  permanent — a bad morning could block valid afternoon trades indefinitely.

FIXES
  - Pattern expiry: patterns that haven't seen activity in 2 hours automatically
    expire. Prevents stale suppression from blocking changed market conditions.
  - Direction stat decay: after 90 minutes of no trades in a direction,
    win/loss counts are halved. Suppression gradually lifts rather than
    staying permanent forever.
  - Correlation cache TTL: cross-market signals now expire after 3 minutes
    instead of 5. Keeps cross-market context current.
  - Fear & Greed: refreshes every 3 minutes (was 5). Catches faster
    sentiment shifts.
  - Live prices: refreshes every 30 seconds (was 60). Velocity calculations
    more accurate with more frequent data.
  - 4-hour auto-reset: every 4 hours the session baseline, pattern cache,
    direction stats, and correlation cache all clear automatically. Prevents
    stale data from compounding over long sessions. Trade history is kept.
    You will see "Auto-resetting session baseline" in the combat log.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 24, 2026 (Afternoon) — Order Direction Bug Fix
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BUG
  When placing NO orders, the bot was sending the wrong price to Kalshi.
  Kalshi's API always expects yes_price to be the YES side price regardless
  of which side you are buying. Buying NO at 35c requires yes_price = 65,
  not yes_price = 35. This was causing some orders to fill on the wrong side.

FIX
  placeOrder() now always converts to YES price before sending to Kalshi.
  Buying NO at 35c sends yes_price = 65 correctly.

CLARITY IMPROVEMENTS
  - Combat log now shows "Firing NO (betting DOWN)" vs "Firing YES (betting UP)"
  - Position badges show tooltip "Betting price goes UP" or "Betting price
    goes DOWN" on hover

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 24, 2026 (Morning) — Defensive Guards + Settlement Fix
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SETTLEMENT BUG
  Bot was reporting wins on positions still open. Kalshi sets the result field
  before paying out — the bot was settling on result alone without waiting for
  the market to be finalized.

  Fix: pollMarketResult() now requires BOTH conditions before settling:
    - result === 'yes' or 'no'   AND
    - status === 'finalized' or 'settled' or 'resolved'
  
  If result is set but not finalized, bot keeps polling every 15 seconds.
  Falls back to expiration_value field if result field is empty.

DEFENSIVE GUARDS ADDED
  - settlePosition: double-settle guard via pos._settled flag. Prevents phantom
    P&L from race conditions between momentum watcher and market poller.
  - renderPositions: null guard if DOM element missing during startup.
  - fetchFills: null ticker guard returns empty array instead of bad API call.
  - analyzeMarketTiming: NaN guard on malformed Kalshi date strings.
  - P&L display: NaN guard shows $0.00 instead of crashing the UI.
  - Momentum watcher: checks pos._settled before attempting exit.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 24, 2026 (Morning) — 6 New Intelligence Signals
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FILL HISTORY ANALYSIS
  Fetches last 50 actual fills per market from Kalshi's /trades endpoint.
  Analyzes YES vs NO volume imbalance, price trend within fills (are prices
  moving up or down in the order flow), and volume spikes (2x+ normal volume
  usually means informed buying). This is real transaction data — much more
  reliable than orderbook snapshots which can be spoofed.

MARKET TIMING
  - Expired markets: automatically skipped before any analysis
  - Near-expiry (less than 3 minutes left): skipped — pure coin flip
  - Fresh markets (less than 5 minutes old): 30% score boost and edge bonus
  - Stale markets: small penalty applied

STRIKE DISTANCE
  For "BTC above $X" markets, calculates exactly how far current price is from
  the strike. Price clearly above strike but YES priced at 40c = obvious
  mispricing worth trading. Price right at the strike = coin flip, skip it.

CROSS-MARKET CORRELATION
  Tracks signals across BTC/ETH/SOL/DOGE 15m markets simultaneously.
  If 2+ correlated markets all signal YES at the same time, logs confirmation.
  If markets diverge, flags the disagreement. Crypto moves together — agreement
  across assets is a much stronger signal than any single market alone.

VOLUME SPIKE DETECTION
  Compares current 24h volume to prior day. 2x+ spike = informed money flowing
  in. Applied as a strength multiplier to fill analysis signal.

5-MINUTE PRICE VELOCITY
  Added standalone signal and hard block:
  - If BTC falls more than 0.3% in 5 minutes, YES bets on BTC are blocked
  - If BTC rises more than 0.3% in 5 minutes, NO bets on BTC are blocked
  - Also used as a positive signal when velocity aligns with trade direction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 24, 2026 (Early Morning) — Parallel Scanning + Signal Quality
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SIGNAL ENGINE REBUILD
  calcQuantEdge() completely rebuilt with strict 2-signal consensus requirement.
  
  10 signals now evaluated per market:
    1. Orderbook imbalance (needs 2x+ for vote, 3x+ for standalone signal)
    2. Trade flow (last 10 trades, needs 75%+ one-sided)
    3. Momentum vs market price (24h change disagrees with current odds)
    4. Fear & Greed contrarian (only at extremes: 15 or below, 85 or above)
    5. Price velocity (5-minute rate of change, 0.2% threshold)
    6. Spread quality (tight spread = high liquidity = reliable signal)
    7. Market price extremes (below 6c or above 94c = likely mispriced)
    8. Fill history (volume imbalance, price trend, volume spikes)
    9. Market timing (skip expired/near-expiry, boost fresh markets)
   10. Strike distance (detects mispricings vs current asset price)

CONSENSUS REQUIREMENT
  Minimum 2 signals must agree OR 1 very strong orderbook signal (3x+ imbalance)
  OR extreme price standalone signal. Single weak signals are ignored.

PRE-TRADE FILTERS (applied after consensus, before Claude)
  - Trend alignment: no YES on assets down 3%+ today, no NO on assets up 3%+
  - Velocity block: no YES if falling 0.3%+ in 5 min, no NO if rising
  - Direction suppression: below 35% win rate after 5 trades blocks direction
  - Pattern suppression: below 30% win rate after 4 trades blocks market+direction

DUAL CONFIRMATION
  Two independent Claude calls must agree before any order fires. If they
  disagree, the trade is skipped. Adds latency but significantly reduces
  false positives.

MARKET SCANNING
  - Parallel scan: all 12 markets scored simultaneously, best picked
  - Markets scored by: consensus strength + fill quality + timing bonus
  - Opportunity scanner: runs every 90 seconds, half normal edge threshold
  - Event-driven: extra scan when asset moves 0.5%+ between checks

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 23, 2026 — Settlement + P&L Math
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SETTLEMENT SYSTEM
  Real Kalshi market polling checks result and status fields every 30 seconds.
  Requires both result AND status = finalized before settling (see May 24 fix).
  Falls back to expiration_value field if result field empty.
  Post-settlement balance reconciliation: fetches real Kalshi balance 3s later.

P&L MATH
  Correct Kalshi payout formula: contracts x $1.00 x (1 - 0.01 fee) - stake
  Stores entryPriceCents on each position for accurate cost basis tracking.
  Sanity cap: P&L cannot exceed contracts x $0.99.
  Session P&L always derived from real_balance - startPortfolio, never
  accumulated locally (prevents drift over long sessions).

BALANCE TRACKING
  fetchBalance() parses balance, balance_dollars, and portfolio_value fields.
  True portfolio = cash balance + open position market value.
  startPortfolio always resets on ARM, never carries from previous sessions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  May 23, 2026 — Risk Management
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CIRCUIT BREAKERS
  - Stop-loss: halts trading if drawdown reaches configured percentage.
    Requires 3+ completed trades AND 5+ balance readings before can trigger.
    Prevents false-positive firing on startup.
  - Consecutive loss circuit breaker: configurable (default 5 losses).
  - Profit lock: halves position sizing once up 5%+ on the session.
  - Recovery mode: reduced sizing during any drawdown period.
  - Max positions cap: never holds more than configured number simultaneously.

KELLY SIZING SYSTEM
  - Quarter Kelly for first 10 trades (conservative ramp)
  - 37.5% Kelly for trades 10-20 (step up)
  - Full requested Kelly after 20 trades
  - Dynamic Kelly: adjusts multiplier based on rolling win rate
  - Profit lock: halves Kelly when profit lock active
  - Recovery mode: halves Kelly during drawdown
  - Hard cap: never more than 25% of cash on single trade
  - Compounding bonus: up to 15% extra sizing from session profits when
    compound mode is enabled

MINIMUM VIABLE STAKES
  $0-2 stake: requires 4%+ edge (higher bar for tiny trades)
  $2-5 stake: requires 2.5%+ edge
  $5-10 stake: requires 1.5%+ edge
  $10+ stake: uses configured edge minimum setting

SELF-DISCOVERING ENDPOINT

ORDER IMPROVEMENTS

KALSHI API NOTES
  Legacy endpoint: POST /trade-api/v2/portfolio/orders
  Required fields: action (buy), side (yes/no), yes_price, count, type (limit)
  CRITICAL: yes_price is ALWAYS the YES side price regardless of which side
  you are buying. Buying NO at 35c requires yes_price = 65, not 35.
  Events endpoint additionally requires: time_in_force, self_trade_prevention_type
